## What Qritique does

Qritique lets you annotate websites and export visual feedback. It works without
a Qritique account or backend service.

## Data processed on your device

When you activate Qritique on a page, it processes page content needed for
annotation and feedback. This includes annotation coordinates and gestures,
notes you write, selected element text and selectors, page addresses, and
preferences such as theme, colors, and shortcuts. Generated feedback text can
include the page title, a cleaned page address, selected element information,
and your notes.

Image export takes screenshots of the browser viewport and may scroll to marked
areas before returning to the original scroll position. It crops the captured
pixels around your marks with surrounding context and stacks separate marked
regions. The intermediate screenshots can include more content than the final
export. Everything is processed locally. Content you annotate or capture can
contain personal or sensitive information; Qritique does not automatically
redact it.

## Local storage and retention

Annotations, notes, and preferences are stored in the browser's local extension
storage. Saved page associations include the page origin, path, and query
string. URL fragments are not part of the storage key. Exported text removes
URL query strings, fragments, usernames, and passwords from its page-address
line. Page titles, page content, and your notes can still contain sensitive
information.

Saved feedback remains locally until changed, removed with extension data, or
removed when the extension is uninstalled under the browser's normal behavior.
Clear markings removes drawings; it does not erase all notes and preferences.
To remove all saved extension data, uninstall Qritique. Copied clipboard content
and downloaded files must be cleared or deleted separately. Qritique does not
save screenshot images in persistent extension storage.

Qritique is disabled in private/incognito windows. It does not load or save
feedback from private tabs.

## Transmission and sharing

Qritique does not upload annotations, notes, screenshots, page addresses, or
preferences to the publisher or third-party services. It has no analytics or
advertising tracking and does not sell user data. Website traffic from pages
you browse and browser/store services are separate from Qritique's extension
data handling.

Copying or downloading feedback happens when you request it. Qritique writes
the selected output to your clipboard or downloads a PNG; it does not read the
clipboard. You decide where to paste or share the output. Clipboard
synchronization and applications you paste into may process it according to
their own settings and policies.

## Browser permissions

Temporary access to an activated tab and script injection provide the
annotation interface. Storage saves feedback and preferences locally.
Clipboard-write access supports export. Both Chrome and Firefox use their
standard visible-tab screenshot API. Qritique does not request debugger,
browser-history, or permanent website-access permissions.

## Use of data

Data is used only to provide the disclosed annotation, local persistence, and
export features. Qritique's use of information received from Google APIs
adheres to the Chrome Web Store User Data Policy, including its Limited Use
requirements. User data is not used for advertising, unrelated profiling, or
creditworthiness decisions.
